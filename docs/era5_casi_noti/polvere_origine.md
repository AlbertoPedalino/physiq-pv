# Origin of the Saharan dust events from the sources (rule fixed on 9 November 2026, before any share was read)

`polvere_origine.csv`: for every Saharan dust event whose source names where the dust was lifted, a rectangle drawn
by us over the named regions (no source gives coordinates) and the days of the lifting, or the 2 days before the
arrival and its first day when the source gives none. polvere_09 (April 2024) is left out: no source read names the
origin.
